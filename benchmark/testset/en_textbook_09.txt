Irregular Plurals
A large majority of nouns in the English language are pluralized by adding an 's' or 'es' to the end of the word. They are called regular plurals. Nouns that do not follow the above rule are called irregular plurals.
Some foreign words form their plurals in the following way.
Singular Plural
syllabus syllabi
axis axes
criterion criteria
index indices/indexes
medium media
curriculum curricula
formula formulae/formulas
Some compound nouns form their plurals in the following way.
Singular Plural
spoonful spoonfuls
passer-by passers-by
by-way by-ways
guest-of-honour guests-of-honour
